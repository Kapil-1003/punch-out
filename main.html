FAQ

Questions

Does the 9 hours include lunch?

Yes. ShiftEnd adds a flat 9 hours to your punch-in time, so any break inside that window is included.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser and nothing is sent or saved.

What if I punch in late at night?

The punch-out time rolls over to the next day and is labelled "Next day".
